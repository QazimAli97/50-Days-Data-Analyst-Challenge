# Executive Sales Performance Dashboard (Excel)

## Overview
This project is part of my **50 Days Data Analyst Challenge** and focuses on building an **executive-level sales dashboard** using Excel.

The objective was not just to visualise data, but to highlight **performance gaps, customer dependency risk, and profitability insights** that support management decision-making.

---

## Dataset
- Regional Sales Dataset (Transactional data)
- Includes:
  - Orders
  - Customers
  - Products
  - Regions
  - Budgeted Sales (2017)

---

## Key Analysis Performed

### 1. Executive KPIs
- Total Sales  
- Total Profit  
- Profit Margin  
- Total Orders  

### 2. Product Performance (Actual vs Budget)
- Compared actual sales with budgeted sales
- Identified top-performing products
- Highlighted budget overachievement patterns

### 3. Customer Contribution & Risk
- Calculated:
  - % of Total Sales (Grand Total)
  - % of Total Profit (Grand Total)
- Compared revenue contribution vs profitability
- Identified customer concentration and margin risk

---

## Key Insights
- A small number of products drive a significant share of revenue
- Strong sales performance can hide margin inefficiencies
- A limited set of customers contributes disproportionately to sales
- Some high-revenue customers generate relatively lower profit

---

## Business Recommendations
- Scale high-performing products to maximise ROI
- Review pricing and cost structures for high-sales, low-profit customers
- Reduce dependency risk by growing mid-tier customers
- Use executive dashboards to shift focus from revenue growth to profitable growth
