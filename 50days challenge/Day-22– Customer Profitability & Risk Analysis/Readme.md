# 📊 Customer Profitability & Risk Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview
This project is part of my **50 Days Data Analyst Challenge**, where I analyze a realistic business dataset end-to-end using **Excel, SQL, and Power BI**.

In this analysis, the focus is on **customer-level profitability**, moving beyond revenue to identify customers that truly add value versus those that create margin risk.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective
To answer a key business question:

**Are all revenue-generating customers actually profitable for the business?**

The goal is to:
- Identify high-value customers
- Detect customers that generate revenue but erode profit
- Support decisions focused on **profitable growth**, not just volume

---

## 🛠 Tools & Techniques Used
- Microsoft Excel  
- Pivot Tables  
- Percentage Contribution Analysis  
- Cumulative Sales Percentage (Pareto / 80–20 principle)  
- Conditional Formatting (Risk Highlighting)  

---

## 🔍 Analysis Performed
- Calculated **Sales Contribution %** and **Profit Contribution %** at the customer level
- Analyzed **Order Volume** to assess operational impact
- Built **Cumulative Sales %** to understand revenue concentration
- Applied **formula-based conditional formatting** to highlight:
  - High-revenue but low-profit (risk) customers
- Segmented customers to support prioritization and decision-making

---

## 💡 Key Insights
- High revenue does not always translate into high profitability
- A small group of customers drives the majority of profitable growth
- Some customers inflate sales figures while weakening overall margins
- Revenue-focused analysis alone can hide profitability risks

---

## 🎯 Business Impact
This analysis helps businesses:
- Shift focus from **volume-driven growth** to **profit-driven growth**
- Identify customers requiring pricing or discount review
- Protect margins without sacrificing revenue
- Enable faster, data-backed decision-making for sales and account teams

---

## 📈 Key Learning
Effective data analysis is not about creating charts — it is about **making insights visible and actionable**.

Understanding **customer efficiency and profitability** is essential for sustainable business growth.
