# Day 46 – Time Intelligence & Trend Analysis (Power BI)

---

## 🎯 Objective

To analyse **business performance over time** and validate growth patterns before building the final interactive dashboard.

This phase focuses on understanding:
- Growth direction
- Performance consistency
- Demand behaviour
- Value vs volume trends

The goal is **analysis first**, not visual polish.

---

## 🧠 Why Time Intelligence Matters

Aggregate KPIs show *how much* business is doing.  
Time-based analysis explains *when and why* performance changes.

Before designing interactive dashboards, it is critical to confirm that:
- Trends are logical
- Patterns are consistent
- Any anomalies are understood

This prevents misleading insights later.

---

## 📊 Analysis Performed

### 🔹 Year-wise Sales & Profit Trend
- Reviewed overall business growth across years
- Identified periods of stability, slowdown, or decline
- Detected partial-period effects impacting totals

This provides a high-level performance trajectory.

---

### 🔹 Monthly Order Volume Analysis
- Analysed order distribution across months
- Observed demand stability and seasonal variation
- Checked for abnormal spikes or drops

This helps understand customer purchasing behaviour over time.

---

### 🔹 Average Order Value (AOV) Trend
- Tracked AOV changes year over year
- Distinguished between volume-driven growth and value-driven growth
- Evaluated pricing and order-size consistency

This adds depth beyond total sales figures.

---

## 🎛️ Design Approach

- Used **rough analytical visuals** intentionally
- Prioritised correctness over aesthetics
- Avoided over-formatting and heavy interactivity
- Focused on validating trends and patterns

Final visual refinement and interactivity will be addressed later.

---

## 🔍 Validation Checks

- Time trends flow logically from past to present
- No misleading spikes caused by date hierarchy issues
- Monthly patterns align with business expectations
- Year-level summaries remain consistent across visuals

---

## 💼 Business Impact

- Reveals growth momentum and slowdown phases
- Supports forecasting and planning discussions
- Helps distinguish demand growth from pricing effects
- Prevents incorrect storytelling in dashboards
- Establishes a solid foundation for executive reporting

---

## 🎯 Key Learning

Good dashboards are built **after** trends are understood.

Time intelligence turns historical data into **planning intelligence**, but only when analysis is validated before presentation.
