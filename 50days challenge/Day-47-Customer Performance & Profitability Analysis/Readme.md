# Day 47 – Customer Performance & Profitability Analysis (Power BI)

## 📌 Project: Sales Analysis  
**Tool:** Power BI Desktop  
**Author:** Mohammad Ali  
**Phase:** Power BI – Customer Analytics  

---

## 🎯 Objective

To analyse **customer-level performance** in order to identify:
- Revenue-driving customers
- Profit-efficient customers
- Differences between customer scale and customer value

This phase focuses on answering a critical business question:

**Who actually creates value for the business?**

---

## 🧠 Why Customer Analysis Matters

Overall sales growth does not guarantee healthy business growth.

Some customers:
- Generate high revenue but dilute profit
- Require high servicing effort
- Reduce overall margin quality

Customer performance analysis helps leadership:
- Prioritise retention
- Improve pricing strategies
- Allocate resources more effectively

---

## 📊 Analysis Performed

### 🔹 Top Customers by Sales
- Identified the **Top 10 customers by total sales**
- Highlighted key revenue-driving accounts
- Provided visibility into customer concentration risk

This helps understand where revenue scale originates.

---

### 🔹 Customer Profitability Comparison
- Compared **Total Sales vs Total Profit** for top customers
- Identified customers with strong revenue but weaker profit contribution
- Highlighted customers delivering balanced scale and efficiency

This reveals differences between **volume-driven** and **value-driven** customers.

---

### 🔹 Profit Margin Analysis
- Ranked customers by **Profit Margin %**
- Identified high-margin customers contributing strong profitability
- Highlighted margin dilution among some high-sales customers

This supports margin-focused decision-making.

---

### 🔹 Customer Order Behaviour
- Analysed **total order volume per customer**
- Identified highly engaged customers
- Differentiated frequent buyers from occasional high-value customers

This provides insight into engagement and cost-to-serve patterns.

---

## 🎛️ Interactivity
- Added a customer slicer for focused analysis
- Ensured visuals respond consistently across selections
- Maintained minimal interactivity to preserve clarity

---

## 💼 Key Business Insights

- High sales do not always translate into high profitability
- Some customers deliver strong margins despite lower revenue
- Customer engagement levels vary significantly across top accounts
- Profitability and order volume are not always aligned

---

## 📌 Business Recommendations

- Prioritise **retention strategies** for high-margin customers  
- Review pricing, discounts, and servicing costs for high-sales, low-margin customers  
- Segment customers into **high-value vs high-volume** groups  
- Focus growth initiatives on customers combining profitability and consistency  
- Use customer-level profitability metrics, not revenue alone, for strategic decisions  

---

## 🎯 Key Learning

Not all customers are equal.

True business growth comes from understanding **customer value**, not just customer volume.

Customer analytics transforms sales data into **strategic intelligence**.
