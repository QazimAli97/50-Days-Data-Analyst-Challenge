# Day 29 – GROUP BY & HAVING | Profitability & Risk Analysis

## Objective
Analyse aggregated sales data using **GROUP BY** and **HAVING** to identify
profitability gaps, performance risks, and optimisation opportunities across
products, customers, and regions.

---

## Business Questions Answered
- Which products generate sales but contribute low total profit?
- Which customers drive high revenue but weak profitability?
- Which regions underperform in terms of profit contribution?
- Which products have high average sales but low margins?
- Which customers place very few orders?

---

## SQL Concepts Used
- GROUP BY
- HAVING
- SUM(), AVG(), COUNT()
- INNER JOIN
- Aggregation-based filtering
- Profitability and risk analysis logic

---

## Tools
- SQL Server (SSMS)

---

## Outcome
This analysis reveals **hidden performance risks** that are not visible at the
transaction level. By filtering aggregated results using `HAVING`, the queries
highlight low-margin products, high-revenue but low-profit customers, and
underperforming regions—supporting data-driven pricing, sales, and operational
decisions.
