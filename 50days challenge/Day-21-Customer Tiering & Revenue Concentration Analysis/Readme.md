## Day 21 – Customer Tiering & Revenue Concentration Analysis

### Objective
To analyze customer-level revenue and profit contribution and segment customers
into value-based tiers to identify concentration risk and growth opportunities.

---

### Analysis Performed
- Calculated customer-wise % contribution to total sales and profit
- Computed cumulative sales % to apply Pareto-based segmentation
- Classified customers into:
  - High-Value (Top ~50% cumulative sales)
  - Mid-Value (Next ~30%)
  - Low-Value (Remaining)
- Compared revenue vs profit contribution across tiers
- Visualized tier-level contribution using Excel charts

---

### Key Insights
- High-Value customers contribute a disproportionate share of revenue and profit
- Profit concentration is slightly higher than revenue concentration
- Mid-Value customers represent the strongest growth opportunity
- Revenue dependency on a small customer group increases business risk

---

### Business Recommendations
- Strengthen retention strategies for high-value customers
- Upsell and cross-sell mid-value customers
- Monitor customer concentration risk regularly
- Optimize engagement strategy for low-value customers

---

### Tools Used
- Microsoft Excel
  - Pivot Tables
  - Calculated Fields
  - Customer Tiering
  - Data Visualization

---

### Key Learning
Customer segmentation based on contribution provides deeper insights than
volume-based analysis and supports more strategic decision-making.
