# Day 50/50 – Final Executive Sales Dashboard (Power BI)

## 📌 Project Overview
This project marks the completion of my **50 Days Data Analyst Challenge**, where I built an end-to-end analytics solution using **Excel, SQL Server, and Power BI**.

The Day 50 deliverable focuses on converting raw transactional data into an **executive-level Power BI dashboard** designed for decision-making, not just reporting.

---

## 🎯 Objective
To design a **business-focused executive dashboard** that answers:
- How is the business performing overall?
- Which products, customers, and regions drive value?
- Where does profitability differ from revenue?
- How can leaders make data-driven decisions quickly?

---

## 🧩 Dashboard Scope (Power BI)

### Executive Summary Page
- Total Sales: **1,235,968,898.8**
- Total Profit: **461,772,325.02**
- Profit Margin %
- Total Orders
- Average Order Value (AOV)
- Interactive slicers for:
  - Region
  - Time (Year / Month)
  - Product
  - Customer

### Analytical Views Included
- Top products by total sales
- Regional sales vs profit comparison
- Customer-level performance and profitability
- Time-based trends and order behaviour
- Drill-through pages for detailed customer analysis
- Slicers synced across all report pages

---

## 🧠 Business Insights Enabled
- Revenue concentration across products and regions
- Profitability differences hidden behind high sales
- Identification of high-value vs high-volume customers
- Regional efficiency comparison
- Decision-ready KPIs for leadership review
