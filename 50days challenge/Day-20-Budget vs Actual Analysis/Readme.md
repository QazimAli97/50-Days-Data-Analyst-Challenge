## Day 20 – Product-wise Budget vs Actual Sales Analysis

### Objective
To evaluate how products performed against their sales targets by comparing
**Actual Sales vs Budgeted Sales**, and assess the accuracy of planning and forecasting.

---

### Dataset
- Sales Orders data (2017)
- Product-level Budget data (2017)
- Total Products Analyzed: 30

---

### Analysis Performed
- Aggregated **Actual Sales** by product for 2017
- Mapped **Budgeted Sales** to each product
- Calculated **% Over Budget** to measure overachievement
- Segmented products by achievement intensity
- Visualized results using Excel charts

---

### Key Insights
- All products exceeded their budgeted targets, indicating conservative planning
- Degree of overachievement varies widely (~300% to 500%+)
- A small number of products disproportionately inflate overall performance
- Strong sales performance can mask weaknesses in budget accuracy

---

### Business Recommendations
- Recalibrate future budgets using historical actuals
- Introduce stretch targets for consistently overperforming products
- Track performance using variance bands instead of binary metrics
- Improve product-level demand forecasting assumptions

---

### Tools Used
- Microsoft Excel (Pivot Tables, Calculated Fields, Charts)

---

### Key Learning
High performance alone does not guarantee effective planning.
Accurate and realistic targets are critical for meaningful performance evaluation.
