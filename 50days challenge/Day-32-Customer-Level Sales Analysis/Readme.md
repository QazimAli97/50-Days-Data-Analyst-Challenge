# Day 32 – Customer-Level Sales Analysis (SQL)

## 📌 Project Overview
This project focuses on **customer-level sales analysis** using SQL Server.  
The objective is to understand **customer contribution, repeat behaviour, and profitability** to support **revenue optimisation and retention strategies**.

Rather than analysing overall sales only, this analysis dives deeper into **who drives revenue, who drives profit, and where business risks exist**.

---

## 🎯 Objectives
- Identify customers contributing the most to revenue and profit
- Analyse repeat customer behaviour using order frequency
- Segment high-value customers using sales thresholds
- Detect customers with high order volume but low profitability
- Evaluate customer purchasing behaviour using average order value

---

## 📊 Business Questions Answered
- Which customers generate the most revenue and profit?
- Who are the most frequent (repeat) customers?
- Which customers qualify as high-value based on total sales?
- Which customers generate high volume but weak profitability?
- Which customers place higher-value orders on average?

---

## 🧠 Key Insights
- A small group of customers contributes a disproportionate share of total revenue
- High order frequency does not always translate to high profitability
- Some customers generate volume but create margin pressure
- Customers with high average order value are often more profitable and valuable
- Revenue concentration creates dependency risk if not managed properly

---

## 💼 Business Impact & Recommendations
- Retain and protect high-value customers through loyalty and personalised pricing
- Review discounting and cost structures for high-volume, low-profit customers
- Focus sales efforts on profitable customer segments rather than pure volume
- Develop mid-tier customers to reduce dependency on a few large accounts
- Use customer profitability insights to guide account management strategy

---

## 🛠 SQL Concepts Used
- `SUM()`, `AVG()`, `COUNT()`
- `GROUP BY` and `HAVING`
- `INNER JOIN`
- `ORDER BY`
- Business-driven filtering logic
- `ROUND()` for financial precision

---

## 🧰 Tools Used
- SQL Server
- SQL Server Management Studio (SSMS)

---

## 🚀 Outcome
This analysis converts raw transactional data into **actionable customer insights**, helping businesses:
- Improve profitability
- Reduce revenue dependency risk
- Optimise retention and pricing strategies
