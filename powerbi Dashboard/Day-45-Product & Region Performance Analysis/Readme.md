# Day 45 – Product & Region Performance Analysis (Power BI)

## 📌 Project: Sales Analysis  
**Tool:** Power BI Desktop  
**Author:** Mohammad Ali  
**Phase:** Power BI – Analytical Drill-Down  

---

## 🎯 Objective

To analyse **product-level and region-level performance** in order to understand **where revenue and profit are actually generated**, moving beyond executive totals into actionable business insights.

This phase focuses on identifying:
- Key revenue-driving products
- Regional contribution to sales
- Differences between scale (sales) and efficiency (profit)

---

## 🧠 Why This Analysis Matters

Executive KPIs explain *how the business is performing*.  
Product and region analysis explains *why*.

Without this breakdown:
- High revenue can hide weak profitability
- Regional dependency risks remain invisible
- Decision-makers lack clarity on where to act

Day 45 bridges the gap between **summary dashboards** and **business actions**.

---

## 📊 Analysis Performed

### 🔹 Product-Level Performance
- Compared **Total Sales vs Total Profit** by product
- Identified top-performing products by revenue
- Highlighted products with high sales but relatively lower profit contribution

This helps assess whether growth is driven by **volume or value**.

---

### 🔹 Region-Level Performance
- Analysed **Total Sales and Total Profit** across regions
- Compared regional scale versus profitability
- Identified regions contributing the largest share of revenue

This reveals geographic concentration and efficiency patterns.

---

### 🔹 Contribution Analysis
- Visualised **regional contribution to total sales**
- Assessed revenue dependency across regions
- Evaluated concentration risk at a high level

Product contribution was analysed at the **product level** due to the absence of product category data.

---

## 🎛️ Interactivity
- Added a region slicer to enable focused exploration
- Ensured visuals respond consistently to filters
- Maintained minimal interactions to preserve clarity

---

## 💼 Business Impact

- Identifies revenue-driving and profit-efficient products
- Highlights regional performance imbalance
- Supports pricing, product, and regional strategy decisions
- Reduces reliance on aggregate KPIs alone
- Enables data-driven prioritisation

---

## 🎯 Key Learning

Contribution analysis does not depend on perfect categorisation.  
When categories are unavailable, **products themselves become contributors**.

Effective analytics adapts to data realities while preserving business meaning.
