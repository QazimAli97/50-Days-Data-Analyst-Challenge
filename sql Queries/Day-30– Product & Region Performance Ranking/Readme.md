# Day 30 – Top vs Bottom Performance Analysis (SQL)

## Objective
Analyse product and regional performance by identifying top-performing
and bottom-performing entities using aggregated sales and profit metrics.
The goal is to support prioritisation, optimisation, and strategic
decision-making.

---

## Business Questions Answered
- Which products contribute the most and least to total sales?
- Which products drive the highest and lowest profit?
- Which regions perform best overall?
- Which regions underperform in terms of profitability?
- Where is performance concentrated vs diluted?

---

## SQL Concepts Used
- GROUP BY
- ORDER BY
- TOP clause
- SUM() aggregation
- INNER JOIN
- Ranking logic for performance comparison

---

## Tools
- SQL Server (SSMS)

---

## Outcome
This analysis clearly separates **high-impact contributors** from
**low-performing entities**, enabling businesses to:
- Focus on scalable, high-performing products
- Identify underperforming products for optimisation or discontinuation
- Allocate resources strategically across regions
- Reduce performance dilution and concentration risk
